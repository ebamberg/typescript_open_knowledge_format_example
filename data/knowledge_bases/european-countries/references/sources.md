# Sources and methodology

## How this bundle was built

Compiled on 26 August 2026. Capitals and land-border/neighbour relationships are stable geographic facts, cross-checked against standard references. Population figures were gathered per-country from national statistics offices (preferred), or UN/EU estimates where no recent national figure was available, generally favouring 2025–2026 data. Each country page cites its specific source and as-of date.

## Known data-quality caveats

- **Ukraine**: no official census since 2001 and no reliable population count since Russia's full-scale invasion in 2022. Wartime displacement, occupied territory, and refugee flows mean credible estimates range roughly from 22 million (government-controlled territory only) to the high 30-millions (projections that don't fully account for displacement). This bundle uses a rough mid-range estimate (~29 million); treat it as indicative, not authoritative.
- **Bosnia and Herzegovina**: no official update since the disputed 2013 census; figure here is a UN/UNFPA estimate.
- **Kosovo**: contested sovereignty. Recognized by roughly 100 UN member states but not by Serbia, Russia, China, or several EU members; not itself a UN member. Included here as a country because it is commonly treated as one in general reference works, with its disputed status noted on its page and on Serbia's.
- **Hungary, Romania**: national statistics offices publish more than one population definition (e.g. resident vs. legal-domicile population); the figure used is noted per-country.
- Several countries' land-neighbour lists include a country outside this bundle's Europe scope (Russia, Turkey, or Morocco) — those are noted as external rather than linked.
