## Notes
Kosovo unilaterally declared independence from Serbia in 2008. It is recognized by roughly 100 UN member states but not by Serbia, Russia, China, or five EU members (Spain, Slovakia, Cyprus, Romania, Greece), among others; it is not itself a UN member. Not an EU member; an official EU candidate since 2024.
