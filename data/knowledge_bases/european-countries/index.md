# Countries of Europe

An OKF (Open Knowledge Format) knowledge base covering 44 sovereign European states: their capital city, most recent available population figure, and list of bordering (land-neighbour) countries.

**Scope:** the standard ~44-country definition of Europe used here includes the EU member states, other Western/Northern/Southern European states (UK, Norway, Switzerland, Iceland, microstates, etc.), and the Balkan/Southeast European states. It excludes countries usually treated as transcontinental — Russia, Turkey, Cyprus, Georgia, Armenia, and Azerbaijan — which are mentioned as neighbours where relevant but do not have their own concept files in this bundle.

Combined population of the 44 countries: approximately **593,107,303** people (figures compiled from national statistics offices, mostly 2025–2026 estimates; see [sources & methodology](references/sources.md) for caveats, especially on Ukraine).
