## Equipment noted on the drawings

SafeGuard LumaExit illuminated exit sign; SafeGuard LumaPath emergency lighting; Ironclad FD60 fire-rated door set with panic-bar hardware (one door set per floor, seven building-wide).

## Notes

Enclosed, fire-rated (60-minute) emergency stairway, pressurised/vented, running the full height of the building in an identical position on every floor. Discharges via a direct exterior door to grade at Floor 1 — walk down only, never up, keeping to the right for two-way flow. Elevators must not be used during a fire alarm; use Stair B instead.
