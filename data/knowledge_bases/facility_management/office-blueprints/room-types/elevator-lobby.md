## Equipment noted on the drawings

ELEV-1 and ELEV-2 — Meridian MVT-3000 traction passenger elevators, 13 persons / 1,000 kg each, 1.6 m/s, centre-opening doors (900 mm clear width). Illuminated exit signage.

## Notes

Shared elevator lobby at the centre of each floor plate; identical position on every floor. Elevators auto-recall to Floor 1 and open their doors when the building fire alarm activates — do not use them during an alarm. The lobby's refuge point is the designated waiting area for wheelchair users and anyone unable to use the stairway during an evacuation.
