# Fire Safety & Egress

Summarised from Drawing A-000 (Legend, Symbols & Building Specification) and the per-floor drawings
A-101–A-107. This page covers what the *drawings* show; day-to-day operating procedure and maintenance
obligations for this equipment are documented in the equipment-manuals and maintenance-contracts knowledge
bases (not linked from here, by design — this bundle does not cross-reference other knowledge bases).

## Fire extinguisher compliance rule

Rule: **1 fire extinguisher per 20 m² of gross floor area, on every floor.**

512 m² floor ÷ 20 m² = 25.6 → **26 extinguishers required per floor**. 26 are shown on every floor plan.
Status: **COMPLIANT (26/26 provided)** on all 7 floors — 182 units building-wide.

Placement (per drawing symbol) prioritises: room entrances, corridors, the kitchen/break room, and
plant/electrical rooms, and within 6 m travel distance of every workstation.

## Egress route

- Every floor has exactly one enclosed, fire-rated (60-minute) emergency stairway: **Stair B**, in an
  identical position on every floor — see [Stair B](/room-types/stair-b.md).
- 2 passenger elevators serve all floors but **must not be used during a fire alarm** — they auto-recall to
  Floor 1 and open their doors when the alarm activates.
- Illuminated exit signage (green running-man symbol, SafeGuard LumaExit) marks the route to Stair B and to
  exterior doors; SafeGuard LumaPath emergency lighting covers corridors, the stairway and the core, and
  activates automatically within seconds of a power failure or fire alarm (≥3 hours battery backup).
- Stair B discharges via a direct exterior door to grade at Floor 1 — occupants walk down (never up) to the
  ground-floor discharge door, keeping to the right for two-way flow, then proceed to the designated
  assembly point outside.
- Floor 1 additionally has a secondary exterior "back door" at the Bin Store / Loading area.
- Wheelchair users and anyone unable to use the stairway should wait at the elevator lobby's designated
  refuge point and await assisted evacuation — do not attempt Stair B unassisted.
