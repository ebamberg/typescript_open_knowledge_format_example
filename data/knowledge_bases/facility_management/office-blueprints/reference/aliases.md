# Alias Names

What a drawing calls a space and what a person actually says in conversation often differ. This page maps
common, plausible everyday phrasing to the canonical room/floor name used throughout this knowledge base, so
an assistant can resolve a casual reference (e.g. "meet me by the lifts") to the correct concept page.

Aliases marked *(from source document)* are phrasing taken verbatim from the blueprint drawings themselves
(e.g. floor notes text). Aliases marked *(inferred)* are common English/UK-office usage for that kind of
space and are not themselves drawn from a source document — treat them as reasonable guesses, not fact.
