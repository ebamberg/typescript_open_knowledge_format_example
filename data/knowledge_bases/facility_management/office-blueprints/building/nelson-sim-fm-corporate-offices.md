## Vertical circulation

- **2 passenger elevators** (ELEV-1, ELEV-2) serve all 7 floors from a shared elevator lobby at the centre
  of each floor plate (core). Rated speed 1.6 m/s, centre-opening doors 900 mm clear width.
- **Stair B**, one enclosed fire-rated emergency stairway, runs the full height of the building, aligned on
  every floor for structural efficiency, and discharges via a direct exterior door to grade at Floor 1.
- The vertical core (elevators + stair + risers + WCs) repeats in an identical position on every floor,
  1–7 (Drawing A-000, General Note 4) — see [Grid & dimension conventions](/reference/grid-system.md).
- Roof level: A/C condenser yard + lift machine room (traction gear) above Floor 7 (Drawing A-900).

## Building section (Drawing A-900)

The 7-storey stack, bottom to top: Floor 1 (Reception/Maintenance) → Floor 2 (Office — Printer) → Floor 3
(Office) → Floor 4 (Office — Printer) → Floor 5 (Office) → Floor 6 (Office — Printer) → Floor 7 (Office) →
Roof (A/C condensers + lift machine room). Central A/C plant on Floor 1 feeds a VAV riser running through
the core; every room on every floor has a supply valve off that riser.

## Fire-safety compliance calculation (Drawing A-000)

Rule applied: 1 fire extinguisher per 20 m² of gross floor area, every floor.

512 m² ÷ 20 m² = 25.6 → **26 extinguishers required per floor**. 26 extinguishers are shown per floor plan.
Status: **COMPLIANT (26 / 26 provided)** on every floor — 182 units building-wide (7 x 26).
