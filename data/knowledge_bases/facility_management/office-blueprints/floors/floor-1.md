# Floor 1 — Reception & Building Maintenance

Drawing A-101. Level 1 of 7. Gross floor area: 512 m².

**Also called:** "Ground Floor", "L1", "Reception", "Floor 1" (see [Alias names](/reference/aliases.md) for the full list).

## Occupancy

Landlord-operated. This floor holds no tenant desks — it houses building reception, security, facilities
management, and the main plant room.

## Floor notes (per drawing)

Ground floor: reception & building maintenance only — no tenant desks. Main reception + waiting + directory; security office (1–3 people); facilities/maintenance office; mail room with pigeonholes; main plant room (chiller/boiler/switchgear) & storage/janitor; staff break area with kitchen; loading/bin store with back door.

## Fire safety

26 fire extinguishers provided on this floor (1 per 20 m², 512 m² floor area) — compliant with the
building-wide rule. See [Fire safety & egress](/reference/fire-safety-and-egress.md).
