# Nelson Sim FM — Office Blueprints Knowledge Base

This knowledge base documents the physical structure of **Nelson Sim FM Corporate Offices**, a 7-storey
Class-A commercial office building at 1 Nelson Sim FM Square, Green Park, Reading, RG2 6GP, UK — floors,
rooms, vertical circulation (elevators/stairs), spatial adjacency ("what's next to what"), approximate
travel distances to core life-safety points, and the everyday alias names people use for these spaces.

Source: Nelson Sim FM office blueprint drawing set (`nelson-sim-fm-office-blueprints/`), architectural record drawings, schematic space-planning diagrams — not construction documents. Distances/positions in this bundle are derived from the drawings' own scale bar and structural grid (40 px = 1 m; 4.0 m x 4.0 m structural module) and are approximate straight-line (label-to-label) measurements, consistent with the drawings' own disclaimer that dimensions are nominal and schematic.

This bundle is self-contained: it does not link to the equipment-manuals, maintenance-contracts, or
lease-agreements knowledge bases, even where the same real-world facts (asset IDs, tenants, vendors)
also appear there.
