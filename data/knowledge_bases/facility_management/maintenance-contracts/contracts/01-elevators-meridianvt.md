# Passenger Elevators (ELEV-1 & ELEV-2) — Maintenance Agreement

Document No. NSF-MC-EL-01 · Rev. A · 2026-09-07. Contract Reference **MVT-NSF-2024-011**.

Customer: Nelson Sim FM Properties Ltd., 1 Nelson Sim FM Square, Green Park, Reading, RG2 6GP, UK (Company
No. 08765432). Contractor: [Meridian Vertical Transport Ltd.](/contractors/meridian-vertical-transport.md).

## Equipment covered

Asset ID(s): **NSF-EL-01, NSF-EL-02**.

Two (2) Meridian MVT-3000 traction passenger elevators serving Floors 1–7

## Included services

- LOLER thorough statutory examination every 6 months by a certified engineer
- Monthly routine lubrication, mechanical inspection and safety-gear check
- Monthly test of in-car emergency intercom and 24-7 monitoring link
- Quarterly door sensor and floor-levelling calibration
- Annual fire-service recall function test, coordinated with the fire alarm testing contractor
- 24-7 breakdown / entrapment response cover

## Exclusions

Excludes damage caused by vandalism, misuse, or force majeure; such repairs are chargeable at the Contractor's standard rates.

## Maintenance schedule

| Task | Frequency | Performed by |
|---|---|---|
| LOLER statutory thorough examination | Every 6 months | Certified LOLER engineer |
| Routine lubrication & mechanical inspection | Monthly | Meridian Vertical Transport Ltd. |
| Emergency intercom / communication test | Monthly | Nelson Sim FM Facilities + Contractor |
| Door sensor & levelling calibration | Quarterly | Meridian Vertical Transport Ltd. |
| Fire-service recall function test | Annually | Meridian Vertical Transport Ltd. |

## Service levels — response times

| Priority | Definition | Response time |
|---|---|---|
| Emergency (entrapment) | Person(s) trapped in a stationary car | 60 minutes, 24-7 |
| Urgent (car out of service) | One car unavailable, building operational | 4 business hours |
| Routine | Non-safety-critical fault (e.g. cosmetic) | 5 business days |

24-7 emergency contact: +44 800 022 9151. Non-urgent requests are logged via the Nelson Sim FM
Facilities helpdesk.

## Fees & reactive callout charges

Annual contract value: £18,500 per annum, exclusive of VAT, invoiced quarterly.

Reactive callouts for entrapment or urgent faults are included within the annual fee. Callouts arising from vandalism or third-party misuse are chargeable at £145/hour plus parts.

Payment terms: invoices payable within 30 days; late payment bears interest at 3% above the Bank of England
base rate. Annual fee reviewable on each anniversary (RPI-linked or by mutual agreement, 90 days' notice).

## Term

3 year(s) from 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2027, renewable by mutual written agreement. Thereafter renews automatically for successive 12-month periods unless terminated on 90 days'
written notice by either party. See [Contract renewal calendar](/reference/contract-renewal-calendar.md).

## Compliance & personnel

Contractor engineers attending site sign in/out via the Floor 1 Security Office and comply with site fire
safety procedures. Contractor maintains public liability insurance ≥ £5,000,000. Governing law: England &
Wales.
