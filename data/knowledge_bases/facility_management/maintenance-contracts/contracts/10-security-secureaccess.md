# Badge Access Control & CCTV — Maintenance Agreement

Document No. NSF-MC-SEC-10 · Rev. A · 2026-09-07. Contract Reference **SAS-NSF-2024-SEC**.

Customer: Nelson Sim FM Properties Ltd., 1 Nelson Sim FM Square, Green Park, Reading, RG2 6GP, UK (Company
No. 08765432). Contractor: [SecureAccess Systems Ltd.](/contractors/secureaccess-systems.md).

## Equipment covered

Asset ID(s): **NSF-SEC-01**.

Sentinel 8 access-control head-end and 12-camera CCTV system, Floor 1 Security Office and building perimeter

## Included services

- Monthly system health check and software updates
- Quarterly CCTV camera cleaning and alignment check
- Annual full access-control audit (user list review, door hardware test)
- 24-7 emergency response for system-down events

## Exclusions

Excludes management of the badge-holder database (Customer's HR/Facilities responsibility) and door hardware covered under the Ironclad agreement.

## Maintenance schedule

| Task | Frequency | Performed by |
|---|---|---|
| System health check & software updates | Monthly | SecureAccess Systems Ltd. |
| CCTV camera cleaning & alignment | Quarterly | SecureAccess Systems Ltd. |
| Full access-control audit | Annually | SecureAccess Systems Ltd. |

## Service levels — response times

| Priority | Definition | Response time |
|---|---|---|
| Emergency (system down) | Access control or CCTV offline building-wide | 2 hours, 24-7 |
| Urgent (partial outage) | Single door/camera affected | 1 business day |
| Routine | Minor configuration change | 5 business days |

24-7 emergency contact: +44 800 664 2299. Non-urgent requests are logged via the Nelson Sim FM
Facilities helpdesk.

## Fees & reactive callout charges

Annual contract value: £7,300 per annum, exclusive of VAT, invoiced quarterly.

Emergency callouts are included within the annual fee; additional configuration change requests are charged at £95/hour.

Payment terms: invoices payable within 30 days; late payment bears interest at 3% above the Bank of England
base rate. Annual fee reviewable on each anniversary (RPI-linked or by mutual agreement, 90 days' notice).

## Term

3 year(s) from 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2027, renewable by mutual written agreement. Thereafter renews automatically for successive 12-month periods unless terminated on 90 days'
written notice by either party. See [Contract renewal calendar](/reference/contract-renewal-calendar.md).

## Compliance & personnel

Contractor engineers attending site sign in/out via the Floor 1 Security Office and comply with site fire
safety procedures. Contractor maintains public liability insurance ≥ £5,000,000. Governing law: England &
Wales.
