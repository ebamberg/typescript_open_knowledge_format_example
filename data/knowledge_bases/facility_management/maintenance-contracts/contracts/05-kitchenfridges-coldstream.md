# Kitchen / Break Room Fridges — Maintenance Agreement

Document No. NSF-MC-FR-05 · Rev. A · 2026-09-07. Contract Reference **CCA-NSF-2024-3**.

Customer: Nelson Sim FM Properties Ltd., 1 Nelson Sim FM Square, Green Park, Reading, RG2 6GP, UK (Company
No. 08765432). Contractor: [Coldstream Commercial Appliances](/contractors/coldstream-commercial-appliances.md).

## Equipment covered

Asset ID(s): **NSF-FR-01 through NSF-FR-07**.

Seven (7) Coldstream CS-400 under-counter fridges, one per floor

## Included services

- Quarterly deep clean, door-seal check and thermostat calibration
- Annual refrigerant and compressor inspection
- Callout repair for any unit not maintaining temperature

## Exclusions

Excludes day-to-day cleaning of fridge contents, which remains the responsibility of floor occupants.

## Maintenance schedule

| Task | Frequency | Performed by |
|---|---|---|
| Interior clean-out & wipe-down (staff-led) | Weekly | Floor occupants (self-managed) |
| Deep clean & door seal check | Quarterly | Coldstream Commercial Appliances |
| Refrigerant & compressor inspection | Annually | Coldstream Commercial Appliances |
| Thermostat calibration check | Annually | Coldstream Commercial Appliances |

## Service levels — response times

| Priority | Definition | Response time |
|---|---|---|
| Urgent (not cooling) | Unit not maintaining 2–5 °C | 48 hours |
| Routine | Noise, minor fault | 5 business days |

24-7 emergency contact: +44 1344 209 900. Non-urgent requests are logged via the Nelson Sim FM
Facilities helpdesk.

## Fees & reactive callout charges

Annual contract value: £2,600 per annum, exclusive of VAT, invoiced annually.

Callouts for units not cooling are included within the annual fee up to 4 per unit per year; further callouts charged at £85/hour plus parts.

Payment terms: invoices payable within 30 days; late payment bears interest at 3% above the Bank of England
base rate. Annual fee reviewable on each anniversary (RPI-linked or by mutual agreement, 90 days' notice).

## Term

2 year(s) from 1 March 2025 to 28 February 2027, renewable by mutual written agreement. Thereafter renews automatically for successive 12-month periods unless terminated on 90 days'
written notice by either party. See [Contract renewal calendar](/reference/contract-renewal-calendar.md).

## Compliance & personnel

Contractor engineers attending site sign in/out via the Floor 1 Security Office and comply with site fire
safety procedures. Contractor maintains public liability insurance ≥ £5,000,000. Governing law: England &
Wales.
