# Central HVAC Plant & Room A/C Supply Valves — Maintenance Agreement

Document No. NSF-MC-HV-02 · Rev. A · 2026-09-07. Contract Reference **NCS-2024-NSF-HV07**.

Customer: Nelson Sim FM Properties Ltd., 1 Nelson Sim FM Square, Green Park, Reading, RG2 6GP, UK (Company
No. 08765432). Contractor: [Northwind Climate Systems Inc.](/contractors/northwind-climate-systems.md).

## Equipment covered

Asset ID(s): **NSF-HV-00, NSF-HV-VAV**.

Central chiller & boiler plant (Floor 1) and 147 VAV room supply valves across all floors

## Included services

- Monthly inspection and service of the central chiller and boiler plant
- Quarterly calibration check of VAV room valves, rotating one floor per visit (all floors covered every 4 quarters, priority floors on request)
- Quarterly replacement of central MERV-13 filtration media
- Annual statutory boiler safety check by a Gas Safe registered engineer
- Seasonal changeover support (heating/cooling mode) each spring and autumn
- 24-7 emergency response for total plant failure

## Exclusions

Excludes replacement of perimeter radiators (Customer-maintained) and any alterations to the distribution ductwork required by a change of floor layout.

## Maintenance schedule

| Task | Frequency | Performed by |
|---|---|---|
| Central plant inspection & service | Monthly | Northwind Climate Systems Inc. |
| VAV valve calibration check (rotating floor) | Quarterly | Northwind Climate Systems Inc. |
| Central filter replacement (MERV-13) | Quarterly | Northwind Climate Systems Inc. |
| Boiler statutory safety check | Annually | Gas Safe registered engineer |
| Seasonal changeover | Bi-annually (Mar / Oct) | Northwind Climate Systems Inc. |

## Service levels — response times

| Priority | Definition | Response time |
|---|---|---|
| Emergency (total plant failure) | No heating/cooling to the Building | 2 hours |
| Urgent (single floor affected) | One floor's VAV valves malfunctioning | 4 business hours |
| Routine (single room) | One room too hot/cold | 3 business days |

24-7 emergency contact: +44 800 774 6612. Non-urgent requests are logged via the Nelson Sim FM
Facilities helpdesk.

## Fees & reactive callout charges

Annual contract value: £32,000 per annum, exclusive of VAT, invoiced quarterly.

Emergency and urgent reactive callouts are included within the annual fee, up to 12 callouts per year; additional callouts are chargeable at £165/hour plus parts.

Payment terms: invoices payable within 30 days; late payment bears interest at 3% above the Bank of England
base rate. Annual fee reviewable on each anniversary (RPI-linked or by mutual agreement, 90 days' notice).

## Term

3 year(s) from 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2027, renewable by mutual written agreement. Thereafter renews automatically for successive 12-month periods unless terminated on 90 days'
written notice by either party. See [Contract renewal calendar](/reference/contract-renewal-calendar.md).

## Compliance & personnel

Contractor engineers attending site sign in/out via the Floor 1 Security Office and comply with site fire
safety procedures. Contractor maintains public liability insurance ≥ £5,000,000. Governing law: England &
Wales.
