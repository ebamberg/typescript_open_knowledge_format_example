# Fire-Rated Stairwell Doors & Panic Hardware — Maintenance Agreement

Document No. NSF-MC-DR-04 · Rev. A · 2026-09-07. Contract Reference **IDH-NSF-2025-1**.

Customer: Nelson Sim FM Properties Ltd., 1 Nelson Sim FM Square, Green Park, Reading, RG2 6GP, UK (Company
No. 08765432). Contractor: [Ironclad Door & Egress Hardware Ltd.](/contractors/ironclad-door-egress-hardware.md).

## Equipment covered

Asset ID(s): **NSF-DR-ALL**.

Seven (7) Ironclad FD60 fire-rated stairwell door sets with panic-bar hardware (one per floor)

## Included services

- Quarterly inspection and adjustment of door self-closers, hinges and seals
- Quarterly function test of panic-bar hardware
- Annual fire-door certification inspection against FD60 rating requirements
- Priority repair of any door that fails to self-close or latch correctly

## Exclusions

Excludes redecoration of door surfaces and replacement of door signage (covered under the SafeGuard Fire & Life Safety Co. agreement).

## Maintenance schedule

| Task | Frequency | Performed by |
|---|---|---|
| Self-closer & seal inspection | Quarterly | Ironclad Door & Egress Hardware Ltd. |
| Panic hardware function test | Quarterly | Ironclad Door & Egress Hardware Ltd. |
| FD60 certification inspection | Annually | Ironclad Door & Egress Hardware Ltd. |

## Service levels — response times

| Priority | Definition | Response time |
|---|---|---|
| Emergency (door will not close/latch) | Fire door compromised | 24 hours |
| Routine | Minor adjustment needed | 5 business days |

24-7 emergency contact: —. Non-urgent requests are logged via the Nelson Sim FM
Facilities helpdesk.

## Fees & reactive callout charges

Annual contract value: £4,100 per annum, exclusive of VAT, invoiced annually.

Emergency repairs are included within the annual fee up to 6 callouts per year; additional callouts are charged at £120/hour plus parts.

Payment terms: invoices payable within 30 days; late payment bears interest at 3% above the Bank of England
base rate. Annual fee reviewable on each anniversary (RPI-linked or by mutual agreement, 90 days' notice).

## Term

2 year(s) from 1 February 2025 to 31 January 2027, renewable by mutual written agreement. Thereafter renews automatically for successive 12-month periods unless terminated on 90 days'
written notice by either party. See [Contract renewal calendar](/reference/contract-renewal-calendar.md).

## Compliance & personnel

Contractor engineers attending site sign in/out via the Floor 1 Security Office and comply with site fire
safety procedures. Contractor maintains public liability insurance ≥ £5,000,000. Governing law: England &
Wales.
