# Managed Print Service — Shared Printers/Copiers — Maintenance Agreement

Document No. NSF-MC-PR-07 · Rev. A · 2026-09-07. Contract Reference **IPO-NSF-MPS-06**.

Customer: Nelson Sim FM Properties Ltd., 1 Nelson Sim FM Square, Green Park, Reading, RG2 6GP, UK (Company
No. 08765432). Contractor: [InkPoint Office Solutions](/contractors/inkpoint-office-solutions.md).

## Equipment covered

Asset ID(s): **NSF-PR-02, NSF-PR-04, NSF-PR-06**.

Six (6) InkPoint MFP-6600 multifunction printer/copier/scanner units (2 each on Floors 2, 4 & 6)

## Included services

- Automatic, vendor-monitored toner and consumable replenishment
- Quarterly on-site service and calibration
- Remote firmware and security updates as released
- Annual deep clean and roller replacement
- Managed print reporting (volumes, cost-per-click) provided quarterly to the Customer

## Exclusions

Excludes paper supply (Customer-provided) and network infrastructure beyond the printer itself.

## Maintenance schedule

| Task | Frequency | Performed by |
|---|---|---|
| Consumable (toner/staples) replenishment | Automatic, vendor-monitored | InkPoint Office Solutions |
| Routine service & calibration | Quarterly | InkPoint Office Solutions |
| Firmware / security updates | As released | InkPoint Office Solutions (remote) |
| Deep clean & roller replacement | Annually | InkPoint Office Solutions |

## Service levels — response times

| Priority | Definition | Response time |
|---|---|---|
| Urgent (unit down, no alternate on floor) | Both units on a floor unavailable | 4 business hours |
| Routine (one unit down) | One of two units unavailable | 1 business day |

24-7 emergency contact: +44 118 977 5599. Non-urgent requests are logged via the Nelson Sim FM
Facilities helpdesk.

## Fees & reactive callout charges

Annual contract value: £14,000 per annum, exclusive of VAT, invoiced quarterly.

The managed print fee is inclusive of standard consumables and service call-outs; excess print volume beyond the agreed annual allowance is billed at £0.006/mono page and £0.045/colour page.

Payment terms: invoices payable within 30 days; late payment bears interest at 3% above the Bank of England
base rate. Annual fee reviewable on each anniversary (RPI-linked or by mutual agreement, 90 days' notice).

## Term

3 year(s) from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2028, renewable by mutual written agreement. Thereafter renews automatically for successive 12-month periods unless terminated on 90 days'
written notice by either party. See [Contract renewal calendar](/reference/contract-renewal-calendar.md).

## Compliance & personnel

Contractor engineers attending site sign in/out via the Floor 1 Security Office and comply with site fire
safety procedures. Contractor maintains public liability insurance ≥ £5,000,000. Governing law: England &
Wales.
