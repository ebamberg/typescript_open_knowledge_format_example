# Main Electrical Switchgear & Building Power — Maintenance Agreement

Document No. NSF-MC-SW-08 · Rev. A · 2026-09-07. Contract Reference **VEC-NSF-2024-EL**.

Customer: Nelson Sim FM Properties Ltd., 1 Nelson Sim FM Square, Green Park, Reading, RG2 6GP, UK (Company
No. 08765432). Contractor: [Voltarc Electrical Contractors](/contractors/voltarc-electrical-contractors.md).

## Equipment covered

Asset ID(s): **NSF-SW-01**.

Main LV switchboard (800A) and building electrical risers, Floor 1 Main Plant Room

## Included services

- Annual Electrical Installation Condition Report (EICR)
- Quarterly thermal imaging survey of the main switchboard
- Annual test of any emergency/backup power provision
- 24-7 emergency response for building-wide power loss

## Exclusions

Excludes tenant fit-out electrical circuits beyond the base-building distribution board on each floor.

## Maintenance schedule

| Task | Frequency | Performed by |
|---|---|---|
| Thermal imaging survey | Quarterly | Voltarc Electrical Contractors |
| EICR statutory testing | Annually | Voltarc Electrical Contractors |
| Emergency/backup power test | Annually | Voltarc Electrical Contractors |

## Service levels — response times

| Priority | Definition | Response time |
|---|---|---|
| Emergency (building-wide power loss) | No power to the Building | 2 hours, 24-7 |
| Urgent (partial loss) | One floor or system affected | 4 business hours |
| Routine | Non-critical fault | 5 business days |

24-7 emergency contact: +44 800 502 7788. Non-urgent requests are logged via the Nelson Sim FM
Facilities helpdesk.

## Fees & reactive callout charges

Annual contract value: £6,800 per annum, exclusive of VAT, invoiced annually.

Emergency callouts are included within the annual fee; call-outs found to be caused by tenant equipment faults are re-chargeable to the relevant floor tenant.

Payment terms: invoices payable within 30 days; late payment bears interest at 3% above the Bank of England
base rate. Annual fee reviewable on each anniversary (RPI-linked or by mutual agreement, 90 days' notice).

## Term

3 year(s) from 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2027, renewable by mutual written agreement. Thereafter renews automatically for successive 12-month periods unless terminated on 90 days'
written notice by either party. See [Contract renewal calendar](/reference/contract-renewal-calendar.md).

## Compliance & personnel

Contractor engineers attending site sign in/out via the Floor 1 Security Office and comply with site fire
safety procedures. Contractor maintains public liability insurance ≥ £5,000,000. Governing law: England &
Wales.
