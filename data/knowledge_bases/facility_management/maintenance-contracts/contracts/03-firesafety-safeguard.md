# Fire Extinguishers, Emergency Signage & Emergency Lighting — Maintenance Agreement

Document No. NSF-MC-FE-03 · Rev. A · 2026-09-07. Contract Reference **SGF-NSF-FE-2025**.

Customer: Nelson Sim FM Properties Ltd., 1 Nelson Sim FM Square, Green Park, Reading, RG2 6GP, UK (Company
No. 08765432). Contractor: [SafeGuard Fire & Life Safety Co.](/contractors/safeguard-fire-life-safety.md).

## Equipment covered

Asset ID(s): **NSF-FE-ALL, NSF-EX-ALL, NSF-EM-ALL**.

182 fire extinguishers, 21 illuminated exit signs, and 49 emergency lighting units across all floors

## Included services

- Annual service inspection and recharge/replacement of all fire extinguishers
- Hydrostatic pressure testing of extinguishers on a 5-year rolling basis
- Annual 3-hour battery duration test of all exit signs and emergency lighting
- Immediate replacement of any discharged, damaged, or tampered extinguisher reported by the Customer
- Support for the Customer's bi-annual fire drill and fire risk assessment review
- Maintenance of extinguisher placement records aligned to the 1-per-20-m² compliance rule shown on Drawing A-000

## Exclusions

Excludes the fire alarm control panel and detection system (covered under a separate Voltarc Electrical Contractors agreement) and structural fire-stopping works.

## Maintenance schedule

| Task | Frequency | Performed by |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly visual inspection (Customer self-check) | Monthly | Nelson Sim FM Facilities |
| Annual service inspection & recharge | Annually | SafeGuard Fire & Life Safety Co. |
| Hydrostatic pressure test (per unit) | Every 5 years | SafeGuard Fire & Life Safety Co. |
| Exit sign / emergency lighting battery test | Annually | SafeGuard Fire & Life Safety Co. |
| Post-use replacement | Immediately after any discharge | SafeGuard Fire & Life Safety Co. |

## Service levels — response times

| Priority | Definition | Response time |
|---|---|---|
| Emergency (discharged/missing unit) | Extinguisher used or removed, floor under-provisioned | 24 hours |
| Urgent (exit sign/light failure) | Exit sign or emergency light not illuminating | 48 hours |
| Routine | Scheduled inspection items | 5 business days |

24-7 emergency contact: +44 800 311 5540. Non-urgent requests are logged via the Nelson Sim FM
Facilities helpdesk.

## Fees & reactive callout charges

Annual contract value: £9,200 per annum, exclusive of VAT, invoiced annually.

Replacement of a discharged or damaged extinguisher is included within the annual fee up to 10 units per year; further replacements are charged at £42 per unit.

Payment terms: invoices payable within 30 days; late payment bears interest at 3% above the Bank of England
base rate. Annual fee reviewable on each anniversary (RPI-linked or by mutual agreement, 90 days' notice).

## Term

2 year(s) from 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2026, renewable by mutual written agreement. Thereafter renews automatically for successive 12-month periods unless terminated on 90 days'
written notice by either party. See [Contract renewal calendar](/reference/contract-renewal-calendar.md).

## Compliance & personnel

Contractor engineers attending site sign in/out via the Floor 1 Security Office and comply with site fire
safety procedures. Contractor maintains public liability insurance ≥ £5,000,000. Governing law: England &
Wales.
