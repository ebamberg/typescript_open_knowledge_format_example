# Meeting Room Video-Conferencing Systems — Maintenance Agreement

Document No. NSF-MC-AV-09 · Rev. A · 2026-09-07. Contract Reference **CLA-NSF-VC-24**.

Customer: Nelson Sim FM Properties Ltd., 1 Nelson Sim FM Square, Green Park, Reading, RG2 6GP, UK (Company
No. 08765432). Contractor: [ClearLink AV Systems](/contractors/clearlink-av-systems.md).

## Equipment covered

Asset ID(s): **NSF-AV-02 through NSF-AV-07**.

Twelve (12) ClearLink Vista 65" displays with PTZ cameras (2 meeting rooms x Floors 2–7)

## Included services

- Monthly remote software/firmware updates
- Quarterly camera and microphone calibration check
- Annual full system service including cabling and mount inspection
- Remote helpdesk support for meeting join issues during business hours

## Exclusions

Excludes the underlying network/internet connection and the corporate video-conferencing software licence.

## Maintenance schedule

| Task | Frequency | Performed by |
|---|---|---|
| Software / firmware updates | Monthly, out-of-hours | ClearLink AV Systems (remote) |
| Camera & microphone calibration check | Quarterly | ClearLink AV Systems |
| Screen & mount physical inspection | Bi-annually | Nelson Sim FM Facilities |
| Full system service | Annually | ClearLink AV Systems |

## Service levels — response times

| Priority | Definition | Response time |
|---|---|---|
| Urgent (room unusable for VC) | Display or camera not functioning | 1 business day |
| Routine | Minor audio/video quality issue | 3 business days |

24-7 emergency contact: —. Non-urgent requests are logged via the Nelson Sim FM
Facilities helpdesk.

## Fees & reactive callout charges

Annual contract value: £5,400 per annum, exclusive of VAT, invoiced annually.

On-site callouts are included within the annual fee up to 6 per year; additional visits charged at £95/hour.

Payment terms: invoices payable within 30 days; late payment bears interest at 3% above the Bank of England
base rate. Annual fee reviewable on each anniversary (RPI-linked or by mutual agreement, 90 days' notice).

## Term

2 year(s) from 1 May 2025 to 30 April 2027, renewable by mutual written agreement. Thereafter renews automatically for successive 12-month periods unless terminated on 90 days'
written notice by either party. See [Contract renewal calendar](/reference/contract-renewal-calendar.md).

## Compliance & personnel

Contractor engineers attending site sign in/out via the Floor 1 Security Office and comply with site fire
safety procedures. Contractor maintains public liability insurance ≥ £5,000,000. Governing law: England &
Wales.
