# Drinking Water Fountains / Bottle-Fill Stations — Maintenance Agreement

Document No. NSF-MC-WF-06 · Rev. A · 2026-09-07. Contract Reference **PFW-NSF-19**.

Customer: Nelson Sim FM Properties Ltd., 1 Nelson Sim FM Square, Green Park, Reading, RG2 6GP, UK (Company
No. 08765432). Contractor: [PureFlow Water Systems](/contractors/pureflow-water-systems.md).

## Equipment covered

Asset ID(s): **NSF-WF-01 through NSF-WF-07**.

Seven (7) PureFlow AquaBar chilled drinking water fountains with bottle-fill, one per floor

## Included services

- Bi-annual filter cartridge replacement across all units
- Annual water quality / hygiene test and certification
- Annual chiller unit service
- Callout support for loss of flow or chilling

## Exclusions

Excludes the building's main potable water supply and plumbing, which remains the Customer's responsibility.

## Maintenance schedule

| Task | Frequency | Performed by |
|---|---|---|
| Exterior clean & sensor check | Weekly | Nelson Sim FM Facilities |
| Filter cartridge replacement | Every 6 months | PureFlow Water Systems |
| Water quality / hygiene test | Annually | PureFlow Water Systems |
| Chiller unit service | Annually | PureFlow Water Systems |

## Service levels — response times

| Priority | Definition | Response time |
|---|---|---|
| Urgent (no water flow) | Unit not dispensing water | 3 business days |
| Routine (not cold) | Chiller underperforming | 5 business days |

24-7 emergency contact: —. Non-urgent requests are logged via the Nelson Sim FM
Facilities helpdesk.

## Fees & reactive callout charges

Annual contract value: £2,100 per annum, exclusive of VAT, invoiced annually.

Reactive callouts are included within the annual fee up to 4 per unit per year; further callouts charged at £75/hour plus parts.

Payment terms: invoices payable within 30 days; late payment bears interest at 3% above the Bank of England
base rate. Annual fee reviewable on each anniversary (RPI-linked or by mutual agreement, 90 days' notice).

## Term

2 year(s) from 1 March 2025 to 28 February 2027, renewable by mutual written agreement. Thereafter renews automatically for successive 12-month periods unless terminated on 90 days'
written notice by either party. See [Contract renewal calendar](/reference/contract-renewal-calendar.md).

## Compliance & personnel

Contractor engineers attending site sign in/out via the Floor 1 Security Office and comply with site fire
safety procedures. Contractor maintains public liability insurance ≥ £5,000,000. Governing law: England &
Wales.
