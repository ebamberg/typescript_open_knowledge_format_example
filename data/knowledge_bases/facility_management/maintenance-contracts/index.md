# Nelson Sim FM — Maintenance Contracts Knowledge Base

This knowledge base documents the 10 equipment maintenance & service agreements covering Nelson Sim FM
Corporate Offices, the 10 contractors who hold them, and the underlying technical asset register (install
dates, warranty status, last/next service) for every piece of covered equipment.

Source: Nelson Sim FM maintenance contracts (`nelson-sim-fm-maintenance-contracts/`, 10 agreements, Rev. A, dated 2026-09-07) and the Nelson Sim FM Vendor & Asset Register (`nelson-sim-fm-vendor-asset-register.xlsx`, compiled 2026-09-07). All companies, contacts, phone numbers, contract references and pricing are explicitly marked SIMULATED / fictional for planning-demo purposes and do not represent real businesses.

This bundle is self-contained: it does not link to the office-blueprints, equipment-manuals, or
lease-agreements knowledge bases, even where the same real-world facts (asset IDs, locations, equipment)
also appear there.
