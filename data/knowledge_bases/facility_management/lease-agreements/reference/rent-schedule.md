Every lease: annual rent payable by equal quarterly instalments in advance on the usual quarter days;
interest of 3% above the Bank of England base rate on rent unpaid more than 14 days after the due date;
rent review on the third anniversary of the Term Start Date and every third anniversary thereafter, to the
higher of the then-passing rent or open-market comparable rent.
