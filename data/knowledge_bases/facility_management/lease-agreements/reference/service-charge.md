# Service Charge

Every lease sets the tenant's contribution at **14.3%** of the Landlord's reasonable costs of operating,
maintaining, repairing, insuring and servicing the Common Parts and Building Systems — the same percentage
in each of the 3 leases on record, consistent with each floor being an equal ~512 m² / 3,584 m² (~14.3%)
share of the building's total gross floor area.

## What it funds

- Maintenance, statutory inspection (LOLER) and 24-7 breakdown cover for the two passenger elevators (Meridian Vertical Transport Ltd.)
- Operation and servicing of the central HVAC plant, chiller, boiler and building-wide VAV distribution (Northwind Climate Systems Inc.)
- Supply, inspection and recharge of fire extinguishers, emergency exit signage and emergency lighting (SafeGuard Fire & Life Safety Co.)
- Maintenance of the emergency stairway, fire doors and panic hardware (Ironclad Door & Egress Hardware Ltd.)
- Statutory testing of main electrical switchgear and building power supply (Voltarc Electrical Contractors)
- Ground-floor reception, security and access-control services
- Cleaning, waste removal and landscaping of Common Parts; and buildings insurance

## Estimates & reconciliation

The Landlord provides a reasonable estimate of the Service Charge for each accounting year, payable
quarterly with the Rent, and reconciles actual costs against estimates within 4 months of each year end.
