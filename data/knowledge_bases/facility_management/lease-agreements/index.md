# Nelson Sim FM — Lease Agreements Knowledge Base

This knowledge base documents the commercial lease agreements in force at Nelson Sim FM Corporate Offices:
which floors are let, to whom, on what terms, rent, service charge, and each tenant's rights and
obligations regarding the building's shared systems.

Source: Nelson Sim FM commercial lease agreements (`nelson-sim-fm-lease-agreements/`), Rev. A, dated 2026-09-07 — 3 executed-form leases covering Floors 5, 6 and 7. No lease agreements for Floors 1–4 were found among the provided source documents; this bundle does not speculate about their occupancy.

This bundle is self-contained: it does not link to the office-blueprints, equipment-manuals, or
maintenance-contracts knowledge bases, even where the same real-world facts (floors, equipment, vendors)
also appear there.
