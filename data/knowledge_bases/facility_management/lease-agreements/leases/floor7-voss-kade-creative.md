# Lease — Floor 7 (Voss & Kade Creative Studio Ltd.)

Document No. NSF-LEASE-F07 · Rev. A · 2026-09-07. Executed as a deed.

Landlord: [Nelson Sim FM Properties Ltd.](/reference/landlord.md). Tenant: [Voss & Kade Creative Studio Ltd.](/tenants/voss-kade-creative-studio.md).

## Premises

The whole of Floor 7 of Nelson Sim FM Corporate Offices (approx. 512 m² GIA), comprising:
one (1) open-plan workspace (approx. 130 m², seating 20 people); four (4) small offices (1–3 people each); two (2) meeting rooms equipped for video-conferencing (8 people each); a kitchen/break room with fridge and drinking water fountain; two (2) further small offices; a quiet/phone-booth room; and men's and women's WCs, all as shown on the corresponding architectural drawing for that floor.

Permitted use: **General office / creative studio use (design & branding services)**. Maximum occupancy: **34 persons**.

## Term

3 years, from 2026-11-01 to 2029-10-31.

Tenant's break option: on 2028-04-30, by giving the Landlord not less than three (3) months
prior written notice (provided all Rent and Service Charge to that date has been paid).

Holding over: if the Tenant remains in occupation after expiry with the Landlord's consent, occupation
continues on a monthly periodic tenancy until determined by either party on not less than 3 months' notice.

## Rent

Annual rent: **£171,520** (£335 per m²), payable by equal quarterly instalments of
**£42,880** in advance. Late payment (>14 days) bears interest at 3% above the Bank of England
base rate.

Rent review: on the third anniversary of the Term Start Date and every third anniversary thereafter, to the
higher of the then-passing rent or open-market rent for comparable local office space (determined by an
independent RICS-appointed surveyor in default of agreement).

## Service charge

14.3% of the Landlord's costs of operating, maintaining, repairing, insuring and servicing the Common Parts
and Building Systems — see [Service charge](/reference/service-charge.md) for the full basis and what it
funds.

## Additional rights

As the top floor, the Premises additionally benefit from roof-level A/C condenser access for supplementary cooling arrangements, subject to Landlord approval.

## Tenant obligations (selected)

- Keep the interior of the Premises in good repair (fair wear and tear excepted).
- Comply with fire safety and evacuation procedures; nominate at least one Floor Warden per 20 occupants,
  trained by the Landlord's Fire Safety vendor, and participate in the bi-annual building fire drill.
- Evacuate via the emergency stairway (Stair B) to the ground-floor assembly point during a fire alarm;
  passenger elevators must not be used during an alarm.
- Do not interfere with, obstruct, or alter any A/C valve, fire extinguisher, exit sign, elevator, or other
  Building System.
- Maintain public liability insurance of not less than £5,000,000.
- May not assign, underlet, or part with possession without the Landlord's prior written consent.

## Landlord obligations (selected)

- Keep the structure, exterior, Common Parts and Building Systems of the Building in good repair.
- Procure that the equipment maintenance contracts referenced in the lease remain in force throughout the
  Term.
- Maintain the Building's fire risk assessment and ensure fire extinguisher provision meets or exceeds 1
  unit per 20 m² of floor area on every floor.
- Quiet enjoyment: the Tenant may peaceably hold and enjoy the Premises without interruption.

## Default & forfeiture

If Rent is unpaid for 21 days after becoming due, or the Tenant is in material breach not remedied within 28
days of written notice, the Landlord may re-enter and forfeit the lease.

Governing law: England & Wales.
