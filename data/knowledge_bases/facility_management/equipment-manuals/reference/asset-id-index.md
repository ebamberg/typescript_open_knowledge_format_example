# Asset ID Index

Asset IDs follow the pattern `NSF-<category>-<number>` (e.g. `NSF-EL-01` = Elevators, unit 01). This index
maps every Asset ID range documented in the equipment manuals to its equipment category.

| Asset ID(s) | Equipment category | Location |
|---|---|---|
| NSF-EL-01, NSF-EL-02 | [Passenger Elevators (ELEV-1 & ELEV-2)](/equipment/elevators.md) | Elevator lobby, core — all floors (1–7) |
| NSF-HV-00 (central plant), NSF-HV-VAV (147 room valves) | [HVAC System & Room A/C Supply Valves](/equipment/hvac-ac-valves.md) | Central plant: Floor 1 Main Plant Room. VAV valves: every room, all floors. |
| NSF-FE-ALL (182 units, 26 per floor) | [Fire Extinguishers — Wall-Mounted ABC Dry Powder](/equipment/fire-extinguishers.md) | Every floor — corridors, room entrances, kitchens, plant/electrical rooms (see Drawing A-000 legend) |
| NSF-EX-ALL (21 exit signs), NSF-EM-ALL (49 emergency lights), NSF-DR-ALL (7 fire doors) | [Emergency Exit Signage, Egress Lighting & Stairway](/equipment/emergency-exit-egress.md) | Stair B and egress routes, every floor |
| NSF-FR-01 through NSF-FR-07 | [Kitchen / Break Room Fridge](/equipment/kitchen-fridges.md) | Floor 1 Staff Break Area; Floors 2–7 Kitchen / Break Room (1 per floor) |
| NSF-WF-01 through NSF-WF-07 | [Drinking Water Fountain / Bottle-Fill Station](/equipment/water-fountains.md) | Floor 1 Staff Break Area; Floors 2–7 Kitchen / Break Room (1 per floor) |
| NSF-PR-02, NSF-PR-04, NSF-PR-06 | [Shared Printer / Copier / Scanner](/equipment/printers-copiers.md) | Print & Copy room — Floors 2, 4 and 6 only (other floors share the nearest) |
| NSF-AV-02 through NSF-AV-07 | [Meeting Room Video-Conferencing System](/equipment/meeting-room-vc-systems.md) | Meeting Room A & Meeting Room B — Floors 2 through 7 (2 systems per floor) |

Source: Asset ID(s) fields stated on each manual's title block.
