# Fire Extinguishers — Wall-Mounted ABC Dry Powder

Asset ID(s): **NSF-FE-ALL (182 units, 26 per floor)**. Quantity: 182. Location: Every floor — corridors, room entrances, kitchens, plant/electrical rooms (see Drawing A-000 legend).

SafeGuard ABC-6 dry-powder fire extinguishers at a density of at least 1 unit per 20 m² of floor area (26 units per 512 m² floor), for use on small, incipient fires only.

## Technical specifications

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Manufacturer / model | SafeGuard ABC-6 dry powder, 6 kg |
| Extinguisher class | ABC — wood/paper/fabric, flammable liquids, and live electrical equipment up to 1,000 V |
| Units per floor | 26 (1 per 20 m² of gross floor area) |
| Total building units | 182 (7 floors x 26) |
| Discharge time | ~10 seconds |
| Inspection interval | Annual visual + service inspection; monthly visual check by Facilities |
| Location marking | Red identification symbol on floor plans; wall-mounted bracket at 1.0–1.2 m height |
