# Shared Printer / Copier / Scanner

Asset ID(s): **NSF-PR-02, NSF-PR-04, NSF-PR-06**. Quantity: 6. Location: Print & Copy room — Floors 2, 4 and 6 only (other floors share the nearest).

Floors 2, 4 and 6 each have a dedicated Print & Copy room with two InkPoint MFP-6600 multifunction laser printer/copier/scanner units under a managed print service.

| Spec | Value |
