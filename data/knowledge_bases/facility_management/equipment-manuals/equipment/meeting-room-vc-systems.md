# Meeting Room Video-Conferencing System

Asset ID(s): **NSF-AV-02 through NSF-AV-07**. Quantity: 12. Location: Meeting Room A & Meeting Room B — Floors 2 through 7 (2 systems per floor).

Both meeting rooms on every office floor (Floors 2–7) are equipped with a ClearLink Vista 65-inch display and pan-tilt-zoom (PTZ) camera for remote video-conferencing.

## Technical specifications

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Manufacturer / model | ClearLink Vista 65" display + PTZ camera |
| Camera | PTZ, auto-framing, 4K capable |
| Audio | Ceiling-mounted beamforming microphone array + integrated speakers |
| Connectivity | Wireless screen-share + HDMI, building network for VC platform |
| Rooms equipped | Meeting Room A & B, Floors 2–7 (12 systems total) |
| Capacity supported | Up to 8 people per meeting room |
