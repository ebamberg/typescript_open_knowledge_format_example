# HVAC System & Room A/C Supply Valves

Asset ID(s): **NSF-HV-00 (central plant), NSF-HV-VAV (147 room valves)**. Quantity: 148. Location: Central plant: Floor 1 Main Plant Room. VAV valves: every room, all floors..

Central Northwind AeroChill 220 chiller and Northwind HydroHeat 150 boiler in the Floor 1 Main Plant Room, distributed through a VAV riser to a Northwind FlowTrim VAV-100 supply valve in every enclosed room and open-plan zone, plus perimeter hot-water radiators.

## Technical specifications

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Central plant | Northwind AeroChill 220 chiller + Northwind HydroHeat 150 boiler |
| Distribution | VAV (variable air volume) riser, 1 per building core |
| Room-level control | Northwind FlowTrim VAV-100 diffuser valve — 1 per room minimum, 147 total |
| Supplementary heating | Perimeter hot-water radiators along exterior walls |
| Room temperature range | 21–24 °C (occupied hours, adjustable ±2 °C locally) |
| Operating hours | 06:30–20:00 weekdays; after-hours on request via Facilities |
| Filtration | MERV-13 central filtration, replaced quarterly |
