# Meeting Room Video-Conferencing System — User & Operation Manual

Document No. NSF-UM-AV-08 · Rev. A · 2026-09-07. Asset ID(s): NSF-AV-02 through NSF-AV-07. Location: Meeting Room A & Meeting Room B — Floors 2 through 7 (2 systems per floor).

## Overview

Both meeting rooms on every office floor (Floors 2–7) are equipped with a ClearLink Vista 65-inch display and pan-tilt-zoom (PTZ) camera for remote video-conferencing.

## Safety information

- Do not touch or clean the display screen with abrasive materials — use a soft, dry microfibre cloth only.
- Do not disconnect or move the ceiling microphone array or camera — report any physical damage to Facilities.
- Do not exceed the meeting room's stated capacity when using the room for in-person + remote attendees.

## Operating instructions

1. Enter the meeting room and tap the touch panel beside the display to wake the system.
2. Select 'Join Meeting' and enter your meeting ID, or use 'One Touch Join' if the meeting is on the room calendar.
3. The PTZ camera will auto-frame the room; use the touch panel to manually adjust if needed.
4. For screen-sharing without a call, connect via the wireless share app or HDMI cable at the table.
5. At the end of the meeting, tap 'End Call' and leave the room tidy for the next booking.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible cause | Recommended action |
|---|---|---|
| Display will not wake | Standby fault or power issue | Check power indicator; report to Facilities if no response |
| No audio on remote end | Microphone muted or network issue | Check on-screen mute icon; rejoin the call if unresolved |
| Camera not framing correctly | Auto-frame needs reset | Use touch panel manual camera controls or restart the call |
| Cannot connect to meeting platform | Network/credential issue | Contact IT Helpdesk; use dial-in number as a fallback |
