# Emergency Exit Signage, Egress Lighting & Stairway — User & Operation Manual

Document No. NSF-UM-EX-04 · Rev. A · 2026-09-07. Asset ID(s): NSF-EX-ALL (21 exit signs), NSF-EM-ALL (49 emergency lights), NSF-DR-ALL (7 fire doors). Location: Stair B and egress routes, every floor.

## Overview

Every floor has one enclosed, fire-rated emergency stairway (Stair B) fitted with SafeGuard LumaExit illuminated exit signage, SafeGuard LumaPath emergency corridor lighting, and Ironclad FD60 fire-rated doors with panic-bar hardware.

## Safety information

- Never use the elevators during a fire alarm — always use the emergency stairway.
- Do not prop open stairway fire doors at any time — they must remain closed to contain smoke and fire.
- Follow illuminated exit signs (green running-man symbol) at all times, even if you know another route.
- If a stairway is smoke-logged, do not enter — use the alternate route or wait for fire service guidance.
- Emergency lighting activates automatically within seconds of a power failure or fire alarm and provides at least 3 hours of illumination.
- Wheelchair users and those unable to use stairs should proceed to the refuge point in the elevator lobby and await assisted evacuation — do not attempt the stairway unassisted.

## Operating instructions

1. On hearing the fire alarm, stop work calmly and proceed to the nearest illuminated EXIT sign.
2. Do not collect personal belongings — leave immediately.
3. Enter the stairway through the fire door; let the door close fully behind you (never wedge it open).
4. Walk down (never up) to the ground floor discharge door, keeping to the right-hand side to allow two-way flow.
5. Proceed to the designated assembly point outside the building and check in with your floor warden.
6. Do not re-enter the building until the all-clear is given by the fire warden or emergency services.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible cause | Recommended action |
|---|---|---|
| Exit sign not illuminated | Bulb/LED or battery fault | Report to Facilities immediately — priority repair |
| Stairway door does not latch/close fully | Closer misaligned or damaged | Report to Facilities immediately — life-safety priority |
| Panic bar difficult to operate | Mechanical wear or obstruction | Report to Facilities; do not lubricate or adjust yourself |
| Emergency lighting flickers on test | Battery nearing end of life | Log with Facilities for vendor inspection at next visit |
