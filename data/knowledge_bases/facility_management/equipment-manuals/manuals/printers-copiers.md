# Shared Printer / Copier / Scanner — User & Operation Manual

Document No. NSF-UM-PR-07 · Rev. A · 2026-09-07. Asset ID(s): NSF-PR-02, NSF-PR-04, NSF-PR-06. Location: Print & Copy room — Floors 2, 4 and 6 only (other floors share the nearest).

## Overview

Floors 2, 4 and 6 each have a dedicated Print & Copy room with two InkPoint MFP-6600 multifunction laser printer/copier/scanner units under a managed print service.

## Safety information

- Do not open the fuser unit cover shortly after printing — internal components can be very hot.
- Do not attempt to clear a paper jam without first switching to standby per the on-screen instructions.
- Do not use non-approved toner cartridges — this voids the managed print service agreement.

## Operating instructions

1. Send your print job from your computer, selecting the correct floor printer queue.
2. Walk to the Print & Copy room and badge-release your job at the printer's touch panel (secure print).
3. For scanning, place the document face-down on the glass or face-up in the document feeder, select 'Scan', choose destination, and press Start.
4. For copying, load your document, select the number of copies and any options (colour, double-sided), and press Start.
5. Clear the output tray promptly and recycle any misprints in the paper recycling bin provided.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible cause | Recommended action |
|---|---|---|
| Paper jam | Misfeed or worn roller | Follow on-screen instructions to open indicated panel and clear gently |
| Job stuck in secure print queue | Badge release not triggered / network delay | Re-tap badge; if unresolved, release from touch panel manually |
| Poor print quality / streaks | Low toner or dirty drum | Vendor is auto-notified via managed print; log a ticket if urgent |
| Scan-to-email not arriving | Network/email server delay | Wait 5 minutes, retry; report to IT/Facilities if persistent |
