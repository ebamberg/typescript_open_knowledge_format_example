# HVAC System & Room A/C Supply Valves — User & Operation Manual

Document No. NSF-UM-HV-02 · Rev. A · 2026-09-07. Asset ID(s): NSF-HV-00 (central plant), NSF-HV-VAV (147 room valves). Location: Central plant: Floor 1 Main Plant Room. VAV valves: every room, all floors..

## Overview

Central Northwind AeroChill 220 chiller and Northwind HydroHeat 150 boiler in the Floor 1 Main Plant Room, distributed through a VAV riser to a Northwind FlowTrim VAV-100 supply valve in every enclosed room and open-plan zone, plus perimeter hot-water radiators.

## Safety information

- Do not cover or obstruct ceiling diffusers, floor grilles or perimeter radiators — this can cause overheating or poor air quality.
- Do not attempt to open, adjust or service the central plant (chiller/boiler) — this is a specialist task for the vendor only.
- Report any burning smell, unusual noise, or water leak from plant equipment immediately and evacuate the plant room area.
- Radiator surfaces can become hot; keep furniture and paper at least 30 cm clear.

## Operating instructions

1. Locate the local thermostat / trim control near the room entrance (where fitted) or use the room's VAV valve default setting.
2. Adjust the local trim dial no more than ±2 °C from the building setpoint; larger changes should be requested via Facilities.
3. Allow 20–30 minutes for a room to respond after any adjustment — VAV systems change temperature gradually.
4. For meeting rooms booked outside normal hours, submit an after-hours HVAC request through Facilities at least 4 hours in advance.
5. If a room feels too hot, too cold, stuffy, or the airflow has stopped, log a ticket with Facilities rather than attempting to adjust the valve itself.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible cause | Recommended action |
|---|---|---|
| Room too warm / too cold | VAV valve out of calibration or local trim maxed out | Log a Facilities ticket with room number and asset ID |
| No airflow from ceiling diffuser | Valve fault or blocked filter | Report to Facilities; do not dismantle the diffuser |
| Whistling / rattling from diffuser | Valve partially closed under high demand | Report to Facilities for VAV rebalancing |
| Burning smell near Plant Room | Potential plant fault | Evacuate area, call emergency line, then Facilities |
| Radiator cold at top, warm at bottom | Trapped air in radiator | Log a non-urgent Facilities ticket for bleeding |
