# Fire Extinguishers — Wall-Mounted ABC Dry Powder — User & Operation Manual

Document No. NSF-UM-FE-03 · Rev. A · 2026-09-07. Asset ID(s): NSF-FE-ALL (182 units, 26 per floor). Location: Every floor — corridors, room entrances, kitchens, plant/electrical rooms (see Drawing A-000 legend).

## Overview

SafeGuard ABC-6 dry-powder fire extinguishers at a density of at least 1 unit per 20 m² of floor area (26 units per 512 m² floor), for use on small, incipient fires only.

## Safety information

- Only attempt to fight a fire that is small, contained, and you have a clear escape route.
- Never attempt to fight a fire that is spreading, producing heavy smoke, or blocking your exit — evacuate immediately and raise the alarm.
- Always position yourself between the fire and your exit route, never with your back to the door.
- If in doubt, evacuate and let trained fire services handle it — no piece of equipment is worth risking your life.
- Raise the alarm and alert others before attempting to use an extinguisher.
- After any use (even partial), the extinguisher must be taken out of service and replaced — notify Facilities immediately.

## Operating instructions

1. PULL the safety pin at the top of the extinguisher handle.
2. AIM the nozzle low, at the base of the fire — not at the flames themselves.
3. SQUEEZE the handle slowly to discharge the extinguishing agent.
4. SWEEP the nozzle side to side across the base of the fire until it is fully out.
5. Back away and keep watching the area in case of re-ignition; evacuate if the fire does not go out within a few seconds.
6. Report the used/discharged extinguisher to Facilities immediately for replacement — use the asset ID on the bracket label.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible cause | Recommended action |
|---|---|---|
| Pressure gauge in red zone | Loss of pressure / possible leak | Report to Facilities; do not use — awaiting replacement is normal, extinguisher is out of service |
| Safety pin/seal broken but not used | Tampering or accidental knock | Report to Facilities for inspection before relying on it |
| Extinguisher missing from bracket | In service, removed, or misplaced | Report immediately; use nearest alternate unit shown on the floor plan |
| Nozzle blocked / no discharge on test | Internal blockage | Do not force; report to Facilities for immediate replacement |
