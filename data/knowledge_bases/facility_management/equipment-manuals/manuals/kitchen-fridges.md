# Kitchen / Break Room Fridge — User & Operation Manual

Document No. NSF-UM-FR-05 · Rev. A · 2026-09-07. Asset ID(s): NSF-FR-01 through NSF-FR-07. Location: Floor 1 Staff Break Area; Floors 2–7 Kitchen / Break Room (1 per floor).

## Overview

Coldstream CS-400 under-counter fridge for staff use, shared by everyone on that floor.

## Safety information

- Do not overload the fridge or block the internal air vents — this reduces cooling efficiency.
- Do not store flammable items, aerosols, or non-food chemicals inside the fridge.
- Unplug before cleaning; never use water directly on the compressor or rear panel.
- Report any burning smell, unusual noise, or refrigerant odour immediately and stop using the unit.

## Operating instructions

1. Store perishable food promptly; label personal items with your name and date.
2. Keep the door closed as much as possible to maintain temperature and save energy.
3. Wipe up spills immediately to prevent odour build-up and mould.
4. Participate in the floor's weekly 'clean-out Friday' — unlabelled or out-of-date items are discarded.
5. If the fridge feels warm or the internal light does not come on, check the plug/breaker first, then log a Facilities ticket.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible cause | Recommended action |
|---|---|---|
| Fridge not cooling | Power/breaker issue or thermostat fault | Check plug is seated; if unresolved, log a Facilities ticket |
| Excessive frost build-up | Door left open or worn door seal | Defrost per label instructions; report worn seal to Facilities |
| Unusual noise / vibration | Unit not level or fan fault | Log a non-urgent Facilities ticket |
| Bad odour inside | Spoiled food or spill | Remove contents, clean with mild detergent; report if odour persists |
