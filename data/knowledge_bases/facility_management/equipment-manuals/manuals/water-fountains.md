# Drinking Water Fountain / Bottle-Fill Station — User & Operation Manual

Document No. NSF-UM-WF-06 · Rev. A · 2026-09-07. Asset ID(s): NSF-WF-01 through NSF-WF-07. Location: Floor 1 Staff Break Area; Floors 2–7 Kitchen / Break Room (1 per floor).

## Overview

PureFlow AquaBar chilled drinking water fountain with an integrated bottle-fill spout and inline carbon filtration, one per floor.

## Safety information

- Do not place your mouth directly on the bottle-fill spout or bubbler — contact use only via a cup or bottle.
- Do not use the fountain if the filter status light shows red/expired — report to Facilities.
- Keep the area around the unit dry to prevent a slip hazard.

## Operating instructions

1. For the bubbler: press and hold the button for a direct drinking stream.
2. For the bottle-fill spout: place your bottle under the sensor; the flow starts and stops automatically.
3. Check the filter status indicator occasionally — green is normal, amber/red means a filter change is due.
4. Report any unusual taste, odour, or discolouration of the water immediately and avoid use until checked.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible cause | Recommended action |
|---|---|---|
| No water flow | Supply isolation or sensor fault | Report to Facilities; use an alternate floor's fountain meanwhile |
| Water not cold | Chiller fault | Log a non-urgent Facilities ticket |
| Bottle-fill sensor not triggering | Sensor misalignment or dirt | Wipe sensor lens gently; report if unresolved |
| Filter status shows red | Cartridge expired | Stop use of bottle-fill/bubbler for drinking; report to Facilities immediately |
