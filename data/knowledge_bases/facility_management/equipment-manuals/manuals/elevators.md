# Passenger Elevators (ELEV-1 & ELEV-2) — User & Operation Manual

Document No. NSF-UM-EL-01 · Rev. A · 2026-09-07. Asset ID(s): NSF-EL-01, NSF-EL-02. Location: Elevator lobby, core — all floors (1–7).

## Overview

Two Meridian MVT-3000 traction passenger elevators serving all seven floors from a shared elevator lobby at the centre of each floor plate.

## Safety information

- Never force elevator doors open. If a car stops between floors, use the in-car intercom and wait for assistance.
- Do not exceed the rated capacity (13 persons / 1,000 kg) shown on the car data plate.
- In the event of a fire alarm, elevators recall automatically to the ground floor — do NOT use elevators; use the emergency stairway instead.
- Do not attempt to force or pry open elevator doors under any circumstances.
- Keep the car threshold clear; report any levelling irregularity (car not flush with the floor) to Facilities immediately.
- Elevators are fitted with a fire-service recall system and automatically return to Floor 1 and open their doors when the building fire alarm activates.
- Wheelchair users and anyone unable to use the stairway should wait in the elevator lobby's designated refuge point and use the intercom to request evacuation assistance during a fire alarm.

## Operating instructions

1. Press the up/down call button in the elevator lobby to summon a car.
2. Wait for the car to arrive; step in once the doors are fully open.
3. Press the button for your destination floor (1–7) on the in-car panel.
4. Stand clear of the closing doors; the door-edge safety sensor will re-open the doors if an obstruction is detected.
5. On arrival, wait for the doors to fully open before exiting.
6. To summon assistance at any time, press and hold the intercom button and speak clearly; the 24-7 monitoring centre will respond within 60 seconds.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible cause | Recommended action |
|---|---|---|
| Car does not respond to call button | Power fault or controller lockout | Try the second car; report to Facilities; do not force doors |
| Doors will not close | Obstruction at door sensor / threshold | Clear the threshold; if unresolved, use intercom to report |
| Car stops between floors | Controller fault / safety interlock | Stay calm, use intercom, wait for assistance — do not attempt self-exit |
| Unusual noise or vibration | Mechanical wear | Take the other car; log a non-urgent maintenance ticket with Facilities |
| Car recalled to Floor 1 with no alarm audible | Fire panel test or fault | Use the emergency stairway; contact Facilities to confirm status |
