# Nelson Sim FM — Equipment Manuals Knowledge Base

This knowledge base documents the 8 categories of building equipment at Nelson Sim FM Corporate Offices that
have a user/operation manual, and the manuals themselves: technical specifications, safe-use instructions,
routine maintenance schedules, troubleshooting guidance, and vendor/emergency contact details.

Source: Nelson Sim FM equipment user manuals (`nelson-sim-fm-equipment-manuals/`), Rev. A, dated 2026-09-07. Every manual states it is "a simulated / fictional planning document prepared for the Nelson Sim FM Corporate Offices demonstration building set. Vendor names, model numbers and contact details are illustrative and do not represent real companies."

This bundle is self-contained: it does not link to the office-blueprints, maintenance-contracts, or
lease-agreements knowledge bases, even where the same real-world facts (asset IDs, locations, vendors) also
appear there.
