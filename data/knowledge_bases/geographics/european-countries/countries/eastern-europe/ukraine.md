# Ukraine

Kyiv

## Population
**29,000,000** (as of 2025-2026 (contested / highly uncertain)), per Mid-range estimate; no official census since 2001 and no reliable count since Russia's full-scale invasion in 2022.

## Notes
Population figure is highly uncertain due to wartime displacement and territory outside government control. Credible estimates range from roughly 22 million (government-controlled territory only, per Ukraine's Ministry of Social Policy, May 2026) to ~30.5 million (Forbes Ukraine, Nov 2025) to UN/Worldometer projections nearer 38-40 million that do not fully account for displacement; 29 million is used here as a rough mid-range figure pending a new census. Not an EU member; an EU candidate country (candidate status granted 2022). Russia is outside this knowledge base's country scope.
