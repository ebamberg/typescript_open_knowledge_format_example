## Notes
Not an EU member; an EU candidate country (candidate status granted 2012). Serbia does not recognize Kosovo's 2008 declaration of independence and considers it part of its own sovereign territory (the Autonomous Province of Kosovo and Metohija); the official Serbian population figure above excludes Kosovo, which is nonetheless listed here as a bordering territory.
